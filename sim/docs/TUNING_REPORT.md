Generated by `make tune`. Do not hand-edit.

Baseline fairness protocol, spec Section 4: *"Each baseline gets a tuning budget equal to ours: a pre-registered grid over its policy parameters, tuned per workload family on validation seeds, evaluated on held-out seeds."*

**Objective:** goodput per provisioned GPU-hour (spec Section 2) -- the primary metric, which already charges the reservation discipline for the capacity its pins strand.

## The protocol

- **Equal budget:** 8 grid points per tunable policy. Enforced by test; the build fails if any grid differs.
- **Validation seeds (tuner sees these):** [1, 2, 3]
- **Held-out seeds (tuner never sees these):** [101, 102, 103, 104, 105]
- **Disjoint:** asserted by test.
- **Cell:** 4 devices x 16 slots, horizon 1500.0s, warmup 150.0s.
- **Untuned by design:** fcfs (the no-control floor has no parameters).

## What this report is NOT

These are TUNED PARAMETERS, not results. The held-out scores here come from a single tuning cell and are not the experiment. The H1 verdict is computed on the full pre-registered grid (spec Section 7) with bootstrap CIs, per-cell best-baseline selection, and the corner-cell exclusions -- that is SG7, and nothing here should be quoted as an outcome.
