## Service-constant sensitivity (+/- 30%)

How far the reservation-vs-niyama_style delta moves when each LOW-CONFIDENCE constant is perturbed on one representative cell (mix 0.3, load 1.0). This quantifies the fidelity caveat (spec Section 6/10).

| Constant | Perturbation | Reservation | Baseline | Delta | Binding? |
|----------|--------------|------------:|---------:|-------|----------|
| single_stream_tokens_per_s | nominal (1.0x) | 22601 | 28793 | -21.5% | binding |
| single_stream_tokens_per_s | minus30 (0.7x) | 21919 | 22933 | -4.4% | binding |
| single_stream_tokens_per_s | plus30 (1.3x) | 25322 | 29113 | -13.0% | binding |
| peak_decode_tokens_per_s | minus30 (0.7x) | 22601 | 28793 | -21.5% | non-binding |
| peak_decode_tokens_per_s | plus30 (1.3x) | 22601 | 28793 | -21.5% | non-binding |
| kv_mib_per_token | minus30 (0.7x) | 22601 | 28850 | -21.7% | negligible |
| kv_mib_per_token | plus30 (1.3x) | 22601 | 28767 | -21.4% | negligible |

Why two constants do not move the result at this cell (4 devices x 16 slots), from an instrumented run:

- **peak_decode_tokens_per_s is structurally unreachable.** Decode throughput is `min(batch x single_stream, peak)`; peak only binds once the batch reaches 40 (= peak / single_stream). The decode batch is hard-capped at `max_concurrent_decodes` = 16; the observed maximum was 16, and across 19342 decode calls peak was the binding term 0 times. Perturbing peak cannot change any decode rate here.
- **kv_mib_per_token never constrains.** Peak HBM occupancy was 39.4% of 80 GiB, so a +/-30% change in KV footprint never changes what fits. The reservation outcome is bottlenecked on the reserved-subset bound and slot contention, not memory, so it is exactly frozen; the baseline's best-effort packing shows a sub-percent response, which confirms the constant IS plumbed through (it is simply not binding). single_stream_tokens_per_s, which sets service time and IS binding, moves the delta as expected.

See docs/SENSITIVITY_HARNESS_CHECK.md for the full determination.
