\section{Degradation probability families}

{\small
\begin{verbatim}
p(H) requirements: p monotone non-increasing in H; p(H) = 0 for H >= H_max;
p(H) = p_ceiling for H <= H_min. Families: piecewise-linear between
(H_max, 0) and (H_min, p_ceiling); exponential p = p_ceiling * ((H_max -
H)/(H_max - H_min))^k for shaping early gentleness; per-class and
per-provenance parameterization as a matrix {H_max, H_min, p_ceiling,
action ladder}_c,tr with lower classes and lower trust assigned earlier
H_max and higher p_ceiling. H computed from the ledger over forecast window
W; W trades responsiveness against stability and is swept in the
evaluation.
\end{verbatim}
}
