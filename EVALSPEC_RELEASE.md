## 0. Pre-registration statement

This spec was written before any simulator or prototype code existed and frozen
before the build began. Hypotheses, metrics, workload sweeps, baseline tuning
procedure, and kill/support thresholds are fixed here, in advance, so results
cannot be quietly reinterpreted after the fact. The kill criterion is a legitimate
scientific outcome: if H1 dies, the paper publishes the negative result
(research-lab posture, D-038) and the patent's commercial narrative is reassessed
(D-049 revisit trigger 1).

## 1. Hypotheses, operationalized

- H1 (spine): Under mixed workloads containing long-horizon agentic flows,
  reservation-with-guarantee scheduling improves TASK-LEVEL GOODPUT PER
  PROVISIONED GPU-HOUR and reduces TAIL FLOW-COMPLETION LATENCY (p95) versus the
  best-tuned preemptive baseline at identical hardware, without violating the
  interactive-traffic guardrail (Section 3).
  Note the normalization: goodput per GPU-hour internalizes capacity stranding.
  Idle-but-reserved capacity directly lowers our own score. There is no separate
  stranding fudge factor to argue about later.
- H2: Headroom-keyed probabilistic degradation yields a superior graceful-
  degradation curve (served value vs offered load) than deterministic class
  demotion (Niyama-style) and preempt-and-requeue (vLLM-style), measured as
  area-under-curve over the load sweep.
- H3: Cross-invocation token budgets prevent runaway-flow starvation: under
  adversarial runaway injections, non-runaway flows retain completion rates
  within a bounded delta of the no-runaway condition, versus unbounded
  degradation under per-request-cap baselines.

## 2. Primary and secondary metrics (definitions fixed now)

- FLOW COMPLETION: a flow finishes all its invocations without abandonment,
  within its deadline class if one exists.
- TASK-LEVEL GOODPUT: value-weighted completed flows per hour. Value weights per
  class fixed in Section 5 (sensitivity analysis sweeps them; headline uses the
  fixed vector).
- PRIMARY METRIC (H1): goodput per provisioned GPU-hour.
- SECONDARY: p95/p99 flow completion latency (agentic classes); p95 time-to-first-
  token and inter-token latency (interactive class - the guardrail); reserved-idle
  fraction (stranding, reported explicitly even though the primary metric already
  prices it); preemption/abandonment counts; wasted-computation fraction (tokens
  generated by flows that never complete).
- H2 METRIC: served-value fraction vs offered load from 0.5x to 3.0x capacity;
  AUC comparison.
- H3 METRIC: completion-rate delta for compliant flows with 0/1/5/10 percent
  runaway-flow injection.

## 3. Kill / weak / support criteria (pre-registered)

Defined over the CORE OPERATING REGION (Section 5 sweep, excluding corner cells
flagged in advance as unrealistic).
- SUPPORT: median improvement in primary metric >= 10 percent vs the best-tuned
  baseline, AND interactive-class p95 TTFT degradation <= 5 percent, in >= 60
  percent of core-region cells.
- WEAK: median improvement 5-10 percent with guardrail intact. Paper reframes as
  conditional result (identifies the workload regimes where reservations pay);
  patent commercial story weakens but filing already made - no action.
- KILL: median improvement < 5 percent, OR guardrail violated across the region,
  OR wins confined to pre-flagged corner cells. Empirical paper publishes the
  negative result with the stranding analysis as the finding. D-049 trigger 1
  fires.
Aggregation rule fixed now: median across cells, not mean (no single-cell
heroics); best BASELINE is selected per-cell (we compete against the strongest
opponent in each condition, not a fixed one).

## 4. Baseline fairness protocol (fixed now)

Each baseline gets a tuning budget equal to ours: a pre-registered grid over its
policy parameters, tuned per workload family on validation seeds, evaluated on
held-out seeds. Baselines:
1. vLLM-style priority + preemption with prefix caching (recompute-on-resume and
   swap-to-host variants).
2. Niyama-style SLO classes with deterministic demotion.
3. CONCUR-style AIMD admission with pause/resume.
4. FastServe-style MLFQ preemptive scheduling.
5. No-control baseline (FCFS continuous batching) as the floor.
Our policy variants (also fixed): strict-pin and tiered-pin (spill with guaranteed
restore) embodiments; K (reserved-subset bound) swept; headroom curve
piecewise-linear with pre-registered thresholds.

## 5. Workload model and synthetic trace suite (the methodology the paper discloses)

No adequate public traces of long-horizon agentic serving exist (checked in T-050
window; re-verify at build time - if a real trace has appeared, it enters as an
additional workload family, logged amendment). Therefore: parameterized
generators, all code and seeds released with the paper.
- FLOW STRUCTURE: invocations per flow ~ lognormal (median 12, p95 80, swept);
  inter-invocation gap ~ lognormal (median 20s tool-time, p95 5min, swept - this
  parameter drives stranding and is the single most important sweep axis);
  output tokens per invocation ~ lognormal; KV footprint grows with accumulated
  context per a fixed growth model.
- MIX: interactive chat traffic (short, latency-sensitive) blended with agentic
  flows at ratios 90/10, 70/30, 50/50 (by token volume).
- ARRIVALS: Poisson base with diurnal modulation and burst episodes.
- ADVERSARIAL FAMILY (H3): runaway flows - loops with unbounded invocation counts
  and heavy token draw, at 0/1/5/10 percent of agentic arrivals.
- CORNER CELLS FLAGGED UNREALISTIC IN ADVANCE (wins here do not count toward
  support): gap medians > 15 min with strict-pin; agentic share > 70 percent;
  offered load < 0.6x capacity.
- Anchoring: parameter ranges justified against published agent-behavior data
  where it exists (agent benchmark step counts, tool-latency reports); every
  anchor cited in the paper, every unanchored range labeled as assumption.

## 6. System model and simulator

Discrete-event simulation at INVOCATION granularity with token-rate service
models (not per-token simulation - fidelity/speed trade-off declared as a
limitation and partially validated in Phase 2):
- Cluster: N devices (sweep 8 to 64), per-device HBM capacity, decode throughput
  model with batch-size dependence, prefill cost model, KV paging with
  host-spill latency model.
- The simulator implements the reservation ledger, admission tests, pinning
  (both embodiments), budgets, headroom computation, and all baseline policies
  behind one scheduler interface (modular by construction - policies are
  plug-ins, per the operator's architecture standards).
- Implementation: Python + SimPy-class DES library; CPU-only; runs on the
  operator's hardware or trivial cloud CPU. Cost of Phase 1: ~zero dollars,
  ~2-3 weeks of build-and-run.
- Validation plan (Phase 2 gate): calibrate service-model constants against a
  small real vLLM run; report calibration error; simulator conclusions carry a
  stated fidelity caveat until then.

## 7. Experiment matrix

- MAIN GRID (H1): {workload mixes} x {gap distributions} x {load 0.7-1.5x} x
  {cluster sizes} x {policies}; ~200-400 cells, 5 seeds each, CIs reported.
- LOAD SWEEP (H2): load 0.5-3.0x on two representative mixes.
- ADVERSARIAL (H3): runaway injection sweep on one representative mix.
- ABLATIONS - mapped one-to-one to patent claim elements (dual-use: paper
  ablation table = prosecution evidence of per-element technical effect):
  A1: full discipline minus cross-invocation budget (per-request caps instead).
  A2: minus non-preemption (reservations preemptible under pressure).
  A3: minus headroom key (queue-depth-keyed drop instead - the direct Q3 test).
  A4: strict-pin vs tiered-pin.
  A5: K sensitivity (reserved-subset bound from 1 percent to 30 percent of
  concurrency).
- SENSITIVITY: value-weight vector, headroom thresholds, forecast-window length.

## 8. Hygiene

Fixed seeds, 5 replications per cell, bootstrap CIs, no post-hoc metric additions
without logged amendment, all generator/simulator/analysis code released with the
paper, results directory append-only.

## 9. Phase 2 - prototype (GATED: H1 support-or-weak in simulation AND provisional filed)

Scope deliberately minimal: reservation ledger + admission + pinning + budget
metering grafted onto a vLLM-class scheduler; single 8-GPU node; two headline
workload cells re-run in hardware; calibration of the simulator; no distributed
implementation for the paper (listed as future work).
Cost estimate (planning only, low confidence): 200-400 GPU-hours on an
8xH100-class rental at roughly 2-3 dollars per GPU-hour equals roughly 3-8
thousand dollars, plus 2-4 weeks engineering. Re-estimate at gate time.

## 10. Limitations declared up front

Synthetic traces (mitigated by released generators + anchored parameters +
adversarial families); invocation-granularity simulation (mitigated by Phase 2
calibration); quality costs of degradation actions modeled via a fixed cost
vector drawn from published cascade/tiering literature including the lab's own
feasibility-condition paper (assumption, swept in sensitivity); single-node
prototype only.

### AMENDMENT 1 (ADOPTED 2026-07-14, operator-ratified at SG6 gate)
A MARS-style baseline (AIMD admission window + MLFQ priority + opportunistic cost-benefit KV
retention, per arXiv 2604.26963) is added to the Section 4 baseline set as policy mars_style,
subject to the identical equal-budget tuning protocol (8 grid points, same validation/held-out
seed split, same objective). Reason: the projected negative H1 headline makes baseline
completeness load-bearing; the strongest predictable reviewer objection to a negative result is a
missing strong opponent. Adopted per the amendment protocol: numbered, dated, reasoned, appended -
never a silent edit.

### AMENDMENT 2 (ADOPTED 2026-07-15, operator-ratified)
All evaluation sweeps run with a measurement horizon of at least 1500s (warmup scaled
accordingly), chosen at the observed stabilization point of the goodput measurement. Reason: the
spec left the horizon unspecified; the build default (300s) was shorter than the median agentic
flow lifetime derivable from Section 5's own parameters (~863s), censoring 78.5 percent of agentic
flows and mechanically biasing the primary metric against reservation-holding policies (measured:
-22.3 percent at 300s vs -7.8 percent at 1500s on one core cell, stable through 6000s). Identified
by the build surface's own diagnostic (H3_ZERO_CHECK.md); ratified BEFORE the verdict grid.
ONE-FIX CLAUSE (operator pre-commitment): this is the sole measurement correction between the
preliminary and the FINAL readout; the D-058 matrix, its thresholds, and all other spec parameters
are unchanged, and the FINAL readout's verdict stands with no further re-measurement in either
direction.

### AMENDMENT 3 (ADOPTED 2026-07-20, operator-ratified)
The main-grid device axis is {8, 16, 32} (the 64-device axis is removed) and replications are 3
seeds per cell. All other parameters, including the Amendment 2 horizon, the load and gap axes, the
policy set, and every Section 3 threshold, are unchanged. Reason: at the corrected horizon the
frozen grid projects to 39,600-49,700 cpu-hours with a single 64-device run at or above 24 hours
wall - unparallelizable and unfixable by budget (SG7_GRID_FEASIBILITY_2026-07-20.md). Scope is
reduced under the amendment protocol; direction-of-effect on the verdict is unknown for the device
axis and neutral for seeds (pilot CIs sub-point at 3 seeds). ONE-FIX COMPATIBILITY: the clause bars
re-measurement of the same quantity; this amendment changes extent, not measurement semantics, and
is adopted before any final-grid data exists. The cluster-size generality claim narrows to 8-32
devices and both papers state it. Spend ceiling raised to 400 USD for the authorized run.
NOTATION (operator-confirmed 2026-07-20, correcting a notation error in the adopting text): "3
seeds" means THREE replications per cell, i.e. the seed set {1, 2, 3} - NOT a single seed valued 3.
This is consistent with the feasibility document's "reduce seeds from 5 to 3" and preserves the
cross-seed bootstrap that Section 3's support and guardrail logic requires. Intent unchanged.

### INTEGRITY RULINGS (appended at packaging, PKG3; part of the citable release)

INTEGRITY RULING 1 (2026-07-20, operator-ratified). 189 manifests from a
pre-Amendment-3 pilot (300-second horizon, 4-device cells) were found in the
verdict path's evidence directory, the consequence of the pilot and the main
grid sharing one results directory. All 189 were QUARANTINED (retained as
evidence at experiments/quarantine_pre_amendment3/, never deleted), and an
input-domain guard was added to the readout: it hard-errors on any manifest
whose horizon, device count, or seed lies outside the grid the readout claims
to report. The contamination biased AGAINST the mechanism (censored-horizon
cells at an excluded cluster size); it was removed on domain logic, not
direction. Measurement semantics unchanged; the guard is regression-tested.

INTEGRITY RULING 2 (2026-07-21, operator-ratified: wall-cap collapse
handling, complete-case pairing). Two baseline runs (niyama_style seed 1 in
one 32-device cell; vllm_style seed 2 in one 8-device cell) reached
congestion collapse and were stopped at the wall-clock cap. Ruling: a
collapsed run carries NO metrics by construction - numbers accumulated up to
a cut-off are the right-censored quantity Amendment 2 exists to exclude - so
collapsed runs are excluded from every aggregate, never imputed. Paired
comparisons in affected cells use complete-case pairing on the surviving
shared seeds. The exclusion FLATTERS the affected baselines (their recorded
numbers come only from surviving trajectories); where the affected policy is
the best baseline, the bias runs AGAINST the reservation discipline. Both
collapses are disclosed in the FINAL readout with their diagnostics.
